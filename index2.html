Yang Zhang

I'm a Postdoctoral Researcher at UConn DSC lab, advised by Prof. Jiong Tang. I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from University of Connecticut in December 2024. My research interests lie in Computational Intelligence, AI-driven Engineering, Digital Twin, Inverse Analysis and Optimization, and Mechatronics Synthesis.

My research follows a three-tier approach: At the algorithmic level, I develop intelligent optimization methods including reinforcement learning-guided strategies, multi-agent coordination, and sparse representation techniques to enhance computational efficiency and accuracy. At the technical system level, I integrate these algorithms with physics-based modeling, sensor technologies, and machine learning frameworks to create robust engineering analysis tools. At the application level, I deploy these integrated solutions to tackle complex problems in prognostics and health management (PHM), metamaterials and intelligent structures, advanced manufacturing, bio-mechatronics, and inverse analysis across diverse engineering domains.
